# home-db-beginner
Beginner with one 2–24 kg dial dumbbell, one band and a station with dip handles. 40 min, 4 days.
progress: carry=goblet-carry@8, chin-up=flexed-arm-hang, core=dead-bug, dip=dip-support-hold, push-up=pushup-floor, squat=goblet-squat@8, vertical-pull=scapular-pull

## Week 1
A Pull and side delts — 36 min
  1. scapular-pull [main] 3 × 5–10 reps
  2. row-single-arm-db [main] 3 × 8–12 reps @ 2 kg
  3. lateral-raise-band [accessory] 3 × 12–20 reps
  4. band-pull-apart [accessory] 2 × 12–20 reps
  5. dead-bug [accessory] 2 × 6–12 reps
B Legs and core — 40 min
  1. goblet-squat [main] 3 × 8–12 reps @ 8 kg
  2. glute-bridge [main] 3 × 10–20 reps
  3. lateral-raise-band [accessory] 3 × 12–20 reps
  4. hollow-hold [accessory] 2 × 10–40 seconds
  5. floor-press-db [accessory] 3 × 8–12 reps @ 2 kg
  reasons: TIME_CUT_DROP(goblet-carry)
C Push and delts — 40 min
  1. dip-support-hold [main] 3 × 10–45 seconds
  2. pushup-floor [main] 3 × 5–15 reps
  3. lateral-raise-band [accessory] 3 × 12–20 reps
  4. press-half-kneeling-db [accessory] 2 × 8–12 reps @ 2 kg
  5. goblet-squat [accessory] 3 × 8–12 reps @ 8 kg
  reasons: TIME_CUT_DROP(triceps-pushdown-band)
D Pull and arms — 35 min
  1. flexed-arm-hang [main] 3 × 5–30 seconds
  2. row-single-arm-db [main] 3 × 8–12 reps @ 2 kg
  3. band-pull-apart [accessory] 2 × 12–20 reps
  4. glute-bridge [accessory] 2 × 10–20 reps
  5. curl-band [accessory] 2 × 12–20 reps
  6. dead-bug [accessory] 2 × 6–12 reps
volume: lats-upper-back 14.6, side-delts 10, rear-delts 4, chest 6, triceps 7.8, biceps 8, quads 6, hamstrings-glutes 9.2, core 6
